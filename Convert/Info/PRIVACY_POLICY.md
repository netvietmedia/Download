## 1. Personal Data We Collect

"Personal Data" means any information that directly or indirectly identifies you. We collect personal data from various sources: directly from you (e.g., when you register or contact support), automatically through your use of the App, and from authorized third parties (such as authentication partners or analytics providers).

We collect the following categories of information:

* **Account Information:** Nickname, profile avatar/picture, and third-party authentication credentials (e.g., Google Sign-In or Facebook Login IDs if you choose to sign in using these services).
* **Communication & Support Content:** Information, inquiries, feedback, or logs provided when you contact our customer support team.
* **Automatically Collected Technical & Usage Data:**
  * **File Transfer Metadata:** Information about file format/type, file name, and file size for transfer coordination. **We do NOT inspect, read, or collect the contents of the files you transfer.** Your files are transferred directly between peer devices over local Wi-Fi / Hotspot networks.
  * **Device Information:** Android ID, Google Advertising ID (GAID), MAC address, hardware model, operating system version, system language, region, and build identifiers.
  * **Network Information:** IP address, network type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G), mobile carrier name, and connection status.
* **Transaction Information:** If you make in-app purchases, redeem rewards, or perform top-up transactions through third-party payment providers, we collect transaction details including transaction ID, order number, amount paid/topped up, contact phone number/email, and associated support inquiries.
* **Approximate Geolocation Data:** Coarse location derived from Wi-Fi SSID and cellular station IDs, strictly required to discover nearby peer devices and establish local high-speed Wi-Fi Direct/Hotspot connections.
* **Notification Permissions:** Information related to notification delivery and system alerts, enabled only with your explicit permission.
* **Installed Applications List (Package Query):** Information regarding apps installed on your device. This is accessed **strictly and exclusively** to enable local app backup, app sharing, and document management requested by you. We do not sell or upload your app list for advertising profiling.

Privacy Policy for Share Now

Effective Date: October 12, 2026 | Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Welcome to Share Now (the "App"), provided by NextAI Team ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data in compliance with applicable global data protection laws (including GDPR, CCPA, Indonesia Personal Data Protection Law, and Nigeria Data Protection Regulation).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and protect your personal data when you use the App, as well as your rights regarding your personal data and how to contact us.

Data Controller: NextAI Team is the data controller for your personal information. If you have questions about this policy, please email us at support@sharenow.app.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect personal information directly from you, automatically through your interaction with the App, and through third-party services:

  • Account Information: Nickname, avatar image, and third-party login credentials (e.g., Google or Facebook ID) if you choose to sign in.
  • Communication & Support: Messages, logs, and inquiry details provided when you contact customer support.
  • Automatically Collected Data:
    • File Transfer Metadata: File format, title, and size for coordinating transfers. We do NOT inspect, read, or collect the contents of the files you transfer. All peer-to-peer file exchanges occur directly over local network channels.
    • Device Information: Android ID, Google Advertising ID (GAID), MAC address, hardware model, operating system version, system language, and locale.
    • Network Information: IP address, network type (Wi-Fi, 4G, 5G), and carrier details.
  • Transaction Information: Order numbers, amounts paid, phone/email, and inquiry logs when using integrated top-up or bill payment partner services.
  • Approximate Location: Coarse location derived from Wi-Fi SSID and cellular station IDs, used strictly for local device discovery and establishing Wi-Fi Direct / Hotspot connections.
  • Notification Bar Permission: Handled only with your explicit consent to show transfer progress and system alerts.
  • Installed Applications List: Information about other apps installed on your device, used strictly and exclusively for local file/app management and user-initiated APK transfers.
